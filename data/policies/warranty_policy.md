# Warranty Policy

Eligible products include a limited warranty covering manufacturing defects for one year from the original purchase date.

Warranty claims should include proof of purchase, product identifier, defect description, troubleshooting steps, and photos or diagnostic logs when relevant.

The warranty does not cover accidental damage, unauthorized modifications, normal wear, consumable parts, or damage caused by misuse.

Replacement approval may require review by warranty operations when the product is high value, the claim history is unusual, or fraud indicators are present.
