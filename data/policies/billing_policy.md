# Billing Policy

Billing inquiries should be reviewed using invoice ID, account ID, payment date, payment method, and subscription status.

Duplicate charge claims require comparison of transaction IDs, timestamps, and invoice records. Pending authorizations should not be treated as completed charges.

Subscription cancellation requests should confirm the effective cancellation date and whether the customer is eligible for a prorated credit.

Chargeback threats, suspected fraud, or billing disputes above the standard threshold should be escalated to the billing operations team.
