# Escalation Policy

Cases should be escalated when they involve legal risk, safety concerns, regulatory complaints, executive contacts, public social media escalation, or repeated failed resolutions.

Agents should also escalate when the customer expresses severe frustration, threatens churn, reports financial harm, or references a previous unresolved case.

Escalated cases require a concise summary, relevant account history, policy evidence, proposed resolution, and risk rationale.

Low-confidence AI recommendations should be routed to human review before any customer-facing response is sent.
