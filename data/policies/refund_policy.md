# Refund Policy

Customers may request a refund within 30 days of purchase when the product is unused, defective, or materially different from the item description.

Refund requests should include the order ID, purchase date, reason for refund, and any supporting evidence such as photos or error messages.

Partial refunds may be offered when the customer received part of the service, used a promotional discount, or kept a portion of the order.

Refunds should not be promised for final-sale items, expired service periods, or cases where abuse or fraud is suspected. High-value refunds require supervisor review before approval.
