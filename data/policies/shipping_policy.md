# Shipping Policy

Standard shipping normally takes 5 to 7 business days after order processing. Expedited shipping normally takes 2 to 3 business days when available.

Customers should receive tracking information after fulfillment. If tracking has not updated for more than 5 business days, agents may open a carrier investigation.

Lost package claims require confirmation of the shipping address, tracking status, carrier notes, and delivery scan. Replacement shipments may be offered after investigation.

Orders marked delivered should be handled carefully. Agents should verify address details and advise the customer to check nearby delivery locations before escalating.
